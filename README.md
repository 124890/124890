## Selected projects

### [Oil storage tank analysis](https://github.com/124890/Floating-Lid-Tank-Depth)

Computer vision research exploring how oil-tank imagery can support inventory analysis. The notebook identifies candidate tank boundaries, shadow contours and floating-lid edges, with visual diagnostics for inspecting detections.

**Libraries:** OpenCV, NumPy, Matplotlib.  
**Status:** exploratory prototype; conversion to validated fill-level estimates is the next stage.

### Oil pricing & alternative data

Private research exploring alternative data for oil-price analysis. The project combines a proposed data-collection framework with experiments in regression, gradient boosting and neural networks.

**Libraries:** pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, Keras, Matplotlib, seaborn.  
**Status:** research prototype · private repository.
